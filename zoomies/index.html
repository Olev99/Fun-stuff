How to play

CareerStart with the Rust Bucket and win coins in story events. Spend them in the Garage on new karts, upgrades, paint and racers. Beat each chapter's rival to open the next one.
SteerHold the phone sideways and turn it like a steering wheel. Gas is automatic.
Keyboard← → steer (tap to nudge) · SPACE drift · E item (hold ↑ or ↓ to throw ahead or behind) · ↓ brake and reverse · Q look back · ESC pause. Gas is automatic. ENTER picks the highlighted button in menus.
ControllerPlayStation, Xbox and Switch Pro controllers work: stick steers, R drift, L item, B brake, Y look back, Start pause. A and B work the menus.
Phone as a wheelTap Use your phone as a wheel on the title screen and scan the code with your iPhone's camera. Tilt the phone to steer and use its big buttons.
Drift + turboHold DRIFT while turning. Sparks go blue, orange, then purple. Let go for a boost.
TricksTap DRIFT just after launching off a ramp. You get a boost when you land.
SlipstreamTuck in right behind another kart for a moment and you get a free boost.
Look backHold the minimap (or Q on a keyboard) to see what's behind you.
Stuck on a wall?Steer away and the kart swings back round. If you're pinned, the drone lifts you out after a few seconds, or pause and tap Back on the road.
Rocket startPress and hold DRIFT right after the 2 disappears in the countdown.
GemsEach gem adds a little top speed, up to 10. Getting hit drops 3.
ItemsDrive through the ? boxes, then tap ITEM to use what you got. For throwables (arrows on the button) swipe ITEM up to throw ahead or down to throw behind, or hold BRAKE while tapping it to throw behind.
Stuck?If you're pinned against something for a few seconds, a drone lifts you back onto the road.
Secret shortcutsEvery track hides one or two. Look for gaps in the wall, stacked crates and odd paths. Some need a boost to be worth it. Once found, they show on the minimap and the track card counts them.
GhostsIn Time Trial your best run is saved as a see-through ghost kart. Race it next time and watch the lap splits: green means you're ahead.
Steering assistSettings has a Light or Strong assist that keeps you off the walls. Handy for younger drivers or while getting used to tilt.
Glide rampsBlue ramps pop out a glider. Steer in the air and land far down the road.
