How to play

SteerHold the phone sideways and turn it like a steering wheel. Gas is automatic.
Drift + turboHold DRIFT while turning. Sparks go blue, orange, then purple. Let go for a boost.
TricksTap DRIFT just after launching off a ramp. You get a boost when you land.
Rocket startPress and hold DRIFT right after the 2 disappears in the countdown.
GemsEach gem adds a little top speed, up to 10. Getting hit drops 3.
ItemsDrive through the ? boxes, then tap ITEM to use what you got.
Secret shortcutsEvery track hides one or two. Look for gaps in the wall, stacked crates and odd paths. Some need a boost to be worth it.
Glide rampsBlue ramps pop out a glider. Steer in the air and land far down the road.
