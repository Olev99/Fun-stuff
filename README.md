# Fun-stuff

- [**Zoomies! Kart Chaos**](zoomies/): a tilt-to-steer kart racer for iPhone, built with three.js. See [zoomies/README.md](zoomies/README.md) for how to play it on your phone.
- [**M4 Lydmotor**](m4-lydmotor/): an engine-sound app for an EV. It synthesises a BMW M4 CSL by default, or an Aston Martin Vanquish S, Aston Martin V12 Vantage S, Ferrari F12berlinetta, Porsche 911 GT3 (991.2), Lexus LFA or Porsche Carrera GT (picked under Innstillinger), in the browser (gear shifts, overrun burble, tunnel reverb), with an output setting tuned for the i4 M50's Harman Kardon system, from phone GPS and motion sensors, with slope compensation, reversing detection (low revs in first gear, "Rygger" in the status line) and a status line for screen lock, audio and GPS; it remembers how the phone sits in the holder (reset under Innstillinger). It has a dark carbon look with a big segmented rev counter, F1-style shift lights and an accent colour that follows the chosen car, and works upright or sideways in a holder. Open it in Safari and add it to the home screen (Share, Add to Home Screen): it then opens full screen as "Lydmotor" with its own icon (a segmented rev counter with a sound-wave glyph). Rebuild with `python3 m4-lydmotor/src/build.py`; checks: `node m4-lydmotor/src/test.js` (sound) and `node m4-lydmotor/src/sensortest.js` (sensor fusion in headless Chrome).
  - Two more cars: Ferrari 812 Superfast and Porsche 911 GT3 RS 4.0 (997). The engine ignores throttle noise from a vibrating holder (no gear hunting), and the i4 HK output has a second treble roll-off above 10 kHz.
