# Game review, November 2026

Four independent read-only reviews of `zoomies/` (engine and netcode, progression and economy, product and player experience, performance and code health). Nobody ran a real iPhone; every finding is from code, node reproductions or arithmetic, and each item says how it was checked. IDs match the reviewers' reports (R1 engine, R2 economy, R3 product, R4 performance). Items marked **[verified]** were re-checked in the code by the director.

## Verdict
- **Strong:** content depth (20 racers, 20 tracks plus reverse, 2 Epics, 5 arenas, 22 items, 22 track events), complete retention loops, careful offline and backup work, defensive input validation (`cleanState`, `cleanCareer`, `parseGhost`, challenge-link bomb guard), cheap CPU hot paths.
- **Weakest:** (1) the economy has holes that break the owner's rules, (2) online play on real phones (message size, no TURN, event clock), (3) what happens when something goes wrong (no error screen, lost cups, silent save failures), (4) GPU budget and memory on iPhone, (5) tests that cannot fail and no CI.

### Batch B: sync ledger (medium-large, engine)
R2-4 / R2-5 / R2-6 / R2-14: the three-way coin merge only works with one shared sync id; across three devices earnings are lost (+500 and +300 merged to 1500, not 1800), a dropped "synced" reply brings back spent coins, a Replace-restore then sync doubles earnings; parts merge by max of "acquired" (selling the same copy on both devices mints its price); one-off rewards pay once per device; version skew can delete new content. Fix: a per-device G-counter ledger `{dev: {earned, spent, caps, capsUsed, acquired, sold}}` merged by per-key max (commutative, associative, idempotent), rewards keyed by reward id, refuse to sync across different stamped versions. Also fixes R2-1 properly.

### Batch E: tests and CI
R4-2 / R4-3 / R4-11: 9 of 19 suites only fail on a crash or timeout (all four quick suites that gate auto-merge); no CI; 21 tests hard-code a Chromium path that is out of date here; `full` takes about 90 minutes, `events` alone 22. Fix: add real assertions with thresholds (shortcut take, finish rates, host/guest agreement, draw-call and triangle budgets), a shared `launch()` helper, a GitHub Actions workflow (stamp check, node tier, quick suite), and a node test tier for pure logic (event schedules ran in 0.63 s in node, all 45 track layouts build in 0.59 s). Also dead exports and a lint step (R4-14), docs drift (R4-15), small per-frame garbage (R4-16).

## Missing features (value versus effort, best first)
1. Pass-and-play party (2-4 players take turns, earlier runs race along as ghosts), S-M.
2. Friends board built from received ghost links (keep challenges per track, race them again, one-tap reply), M.
3. Invite link and one-tap online rematch, S-M.
4. Incoming-attack warnings at the screen edge (also helps sound-off play), S-M.
5. Custom rules for Quick Race and the lobby (laps, item set, events, mirror), M.
6. Accessibility pack (text size, swapped sides, button size, remapping, drift pips, high-contrast HUD), M.
7. Resume interrupted cups, M.
8. Photo mode (orbit camera, hidden HUD, share as file), M.
9. Post-League goals (Mirror Cups, a 20-track Zoomie Gauntlet, reverse-track medals), M.
10. Seasons by date (monthly theme, Weekly twist set, two seasonal hats), M.
11. Replay of your last race, L.
12. Translation (about 1,000 strings, fixed `en-US` formats, Latin-only fonts), L.
Not suggested: split-screen on one Mac (two cameras and HUDs, L); pass-and-play gets most of the value.

## Things nobody could verify (need a real iPhone)
- Frame rate, thermals and the GPU memory numbers (calculated, not measured), the resolution stutter (modelled), screen density at 852x393.
- Whether iOS wipes a non-installed site's save after about 7 days (backup nudge is every 14 days) and how far `storage.persist()` protects it.
- Unordered-channel and TURN behaviour on carrier networks.
- Reviewer suspicions not confirmed: unbounded PeerJS send queue on slow links, a never-shrinking kart geometry cache, a heavy Career save passing 16.3 KB, gem-event 3-star thresholds above the gems on the track, c2-3 Coral feasibility in the Rust Bucket.

## Performance budget to adopt
95% of frames at 16.7 ms or less and under 1% over 33 ms on odyssey and maple (iPhone 15 Pro, Safari Web Inspector); GPU 12 ms or less at Auto; a 15-minute session (3 min menus, 4 races) at 55 fps or more with resolution 1.7x or more; at most 250 main-pass and 120 shadow-pass draw calls and 600k visible triangles per track from fixed cameras (assert in `tracks`); render targets 130 MB or less at Auto and flat across 10 race starts; 1 MB gzip or less on first visit, title in 3 s on 4G; `race.update` 2 ms or less for 8 karts; 40 or fewer audio sources.
